    For the grown-ups: how to host

    Share this window on Zoom. Ask children to say the letter on a card and its new position: “Move C to 1!” One host controls the game; no participant login is needed.

    Let a different child explain each move. Nothing is timed and nobody is eliminated. After each story, invite five children to retell one picture each.

    The three Ramayana rounds are short, age-appropriate retellings. The Ganesha race follows the commonly told Ganesha–Kartikeya race story, with Artisans Crest as the main reference. “Ganesha the scribe” is presented as a popular/traditional retelling; versions differ.

    Made for curious minds. Best played together.
