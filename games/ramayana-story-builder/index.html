    For the grown-ups: how to host

    Share this page on Zoom. Let children call out a card letter and destination number, for example “Move C to 1.” No timer and no elimination. After solving, ask children to retell the five pictures.

    The three Ramayana rounds are short retellings. The Ganesha race follows Artisans Crest’s Ganesh and Karthik story. “Ganesha the scribe” is presented as a popular/traditional story; versions of this episode differ. A child-oriented version is available from Katha Kids.
